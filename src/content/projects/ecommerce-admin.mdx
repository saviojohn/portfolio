## Key Capabilities

- **Orders & Shipment Tracker**: Comprehensive order list & detail views supporting shipment partner updates, tracking numbers, and interactive status timelines.
- **Promotions & Sales CRUD**: Form engine managing promotional banners, handle handle slug generation, stackable discount flags, and SEO metadata.
- **Invoice Blob Exporter**: Client-side blob fetching and download generation for customer invoice PDFs.
