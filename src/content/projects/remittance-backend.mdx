## Key Capabilities

- **FCM Push Dispatch Pipeline**: RabbitMQ queue consumer that formats and delivers iOS APNs & Android FCM payloads asynchronously.
- **Smart Notification Suppressor**: Automatically suppresses tray push alerts when a customer is currently active inside the chat screen.
- **Device Hygiene & TTL**: Background cleanup worker enforcing 90-day device record TTL and removing invalidated APNs/FCM tokens.
- **Transactional SES Emailing**: Integrated AWS SES via boto3 with HTML formatting and CID-embedded branding.
